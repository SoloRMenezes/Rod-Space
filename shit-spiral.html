Build your spiral.

  1. Everyone starts with three rated situations, ordered from least bad to worst.
  2. On your turn, read the new situation. Its number stays hidden. Tap the gap where you think it belongs in your lane.
  3. Correct? Keep it. Wrong? The next player tries the same card in their own lane, without seeing its number.
  4. If everyone misses, reveal it and discard it. The original player's next neighbour starts the next round.
  5. First to ten cards wins. If the deck runs out, most cards wins.

Numbers are subjective. Arguing about them is half the fun. Nothing here is an expert assessment of suffering.
