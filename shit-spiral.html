How to play

  1. You begin with three cards ordered from least to most miserable.
  2. The new card hides its score. Tap two neighbouring cards to place it between them.
  3. For the two outer gaps, tap the first card twice to place before it or the last card twice to place after it.
  4. Correct guesses join your lane. In multiplayer, a missed card passes to the next player without revealing its score.
  5. First to ten cards wins. Solo has unlimited attempts.
  6. LAN mode gives both devices the same challenge while each player keeps a separate lane.
