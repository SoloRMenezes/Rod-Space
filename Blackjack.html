21 CHALLENGE

Strategic Card Game

How to Play

  • 1. Reach a total score of 21 or as close as possible without going over.
  • 2. Number cards are face value. Face cards (J, Q, K) are 10. Aces are 1 or 11.
  • 3. 'Hit' to take another card, 'Stand' to keep your current hand.
  • 4. Beat the house score to win the round!
