How to play

Tap two cards to reveal their symbols. Match a pair and they stay face up. Miss, and both turn back over. In solo play, find every pair with the fewest moves. Against other players, a match earns one point and another turn; a miss passes the turn. Most pairs wins, and equal top scores are a draw. Multiplayer uses the same device. The CPU only remembers cards that have been revealed. Only solo with CPU off tracks your time and moves. The timer starts on your first flip. Use Tab and Enter or Space to play with a keyboard.
