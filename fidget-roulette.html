How to play

  1. Tap any number or outside bet to place 10 coins.
  2. Tap again to add another 10 coins to that bet.
  3. Press the centre of the wheel to spin.
  4. Straight numbers pay 36×, dozens pay 3× and red/black, odd/even or high/low pay 2×.
