How it works

Reps from all sets add up for the local calendar day. Your minimum stays fixed until your streak is lost. The next maximum test sets a new minimum.

Every Monday adds one freeze, starting the week you join if you join on Monday. Each freeze protects one missed day. Protected days preserve your streak; completed days increase it.
