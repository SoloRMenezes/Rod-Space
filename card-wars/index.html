The digital game flow

How to Play

Build a compatible Creature deck, place cards across four Landscapes, and defeat the opposing Hero lane by lane.

1

Set up your deck

Open Decks and build a deck of up to 40 cards, including four owned Landscape cards, one for each numbered lane. Fill the remaining slots with playable Creature cards, including Rainbow cards or Creatures that match those Landscapes.

2

Start a battle

Press Play from the home screen. You and the opponent draw five cards. The starting player is chosen at random, and the battle begins with 6 Energy and 2 Actions.

3

Spend Energy to play

Drag a card from your hand onto an open lane with a matching Landscape. Rainbow Creatures can use any lane. The card's cost uses Energy and each placement uses 1 Action.

4

Use your Actions

You have 2 Actions each turn. Playing a Creature costs 1 Action. You can spend 1 Action on Draw to take an extra card, or finish the turn with End Turn.

5

Attack an enemy Creature

Drag one of your cards on the board onto an opposing Creature. Each Creature can attack once per round. Its ATK damages the target, and the target strikes back with its ATK unless it already countered this round.

6

Break through a lane

After the opening round, a Creature can hit the enemy Hero directly when there are no opposing Creatures on the board. Direct attacks deal the Creature's ATK to the enemy HP.

7

Refill and collect

End Turn gives the opponent a phase, then your Energy refills to 6 for the next round. Energy never goes above 6, and you always have exactly 2 Actions each turn. You draw one card each new round. Win battles for coins, levels, streak rewards, and achievements.

What is playable right now?

This prototype's battle engine supports Creature cards only. Buildings and Spells can be collected, but they are marked Not playable yet and cannot be added to a battle deck. A card marked Wrong landscape needs a matching Landscape in one of your four lanes.
